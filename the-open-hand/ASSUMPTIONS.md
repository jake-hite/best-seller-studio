# ASSUMPTIONS

This project draws its entire magic system, world, and metaphysics from the
`jake-hite/novel-bible` repository (read in full: permeance.md, The-schism.md,
World-Implication.md, antagonist.md, character-types.md,
Progtanists-Driver.md, story-structure-research.md, story-structure.md, and
the companion doc matriarchy-and-gendered-permeability.md, authored as part
of this project to answer a question those documents left open). Nothing
below contradicts that source material; where the bible left a question
explicitly open, this file records the decision made to close it.

## Inferred from the task prompt

- **Language:** English.
- **Genre:** Secondary-world fantasy, adult/upper-YA crossover register. Low
  grimdark — the world has real stakes and a real institutional flaw, but the
  tonal target is warm, funny, and emotionally generous, not bleak.
- **Audience:** Readers of character-forward fantasy who want a thoughtfully
  built matriarchy and a protagonist who is allowed to be uncool. Comp space:
  readers of Naomi Novik's Uprooted/Spinning Silver, T. Kingfisher, Robin
  McKinley — cozy-adjacent competence porn with a real thematic spine, not
  grimdark political fantasy and not a romance-first market entry.
- **Target length:** Full novel, ~95,000–105,000 words, 20 chapters. (Per
  book-genesis-codex norms and the bible's own structural documents, which
  map a 7-point structure across roughly that chapter count.)
- **Narrative mode:** Third-person limited, single POV (Wren), past tense.
  Close, voicey, wry — the prose should sound like Wren's interior monologue
  even in description.
- **Market position:** Mid-list debut fantasy, not epic-series-opener framed.
  Stands alone with room for a sequel, doesn't require one.

## Decisions made to close questions the bible left open

1. **The Frontier Question's "Open Decision" (does the beloved live or die)
   is resolved: the beloved lives, but the rescue is costly and changes both
   sisters permanently.** Chosen because the user explicitly asked for a
   book that lets its female lead be goofy and warm rather than grim; a dead
   sister pushes the book toward tragedy-coded grimdark the brief doesn't
   ask for. The cost is paid in identity and permanent vulnerability, not in
   a body count.
2. **The "Recommended Braid" from Progtanists-Driver.md is adopted directly:**
   protagonist wants to save a dying beloved (her sister); the only source of
   the knowledge that can do it is the antagonist, who will teach the
   Severance as the price (or, more precisely, whose own survival is proof
   the Severance "works," which is the temptation).
3. **The antagonist's route to villainy is the Broken Healer** (per
   character-types.md's three named routes), specifically chosen because it
   lets the antagonist be the dark mirror of the matriarchy's own prestige
   ideal described in matriarchy-and-gendered-permeability.md — she is what
   happens to a woman who succeeded perfectly at "luminous, composed, never
   visibly overwhelmed" until the math stopped working. This makes her
   sympathetic and "right" about the diagnosis (the ideal is a body count)
   while being wrong and dangerous about the cure (Sever everyone before it
   happens to them, with herself as the necessary, controlling hub).
4. **The protagonist is a Material-opener, not a Person-opener.** The bible
   identifies Material-opening as the least psychologically burdened
   specialization and the gender-mixed trade caste. Putting the protagonist
   there — rather than in the prestige Person-opener/Court track the
   matriarchy formally rewards — is the mechanism for "female lead allowed to
   be goofy": she is, by Aldermere's own social logic, a structurally minor
   character in her own society, which is the joke and the point.
5. **Wyrms (introduced in the matriarchy companion doc) are the mechanism
   that makes the dragon-nerd trait load-bearing rather than decorative.**
   Wyrm material is established as the single most resonance-dense
   alchemical substance in the world. The rescue plot requires it. The
   protagonist's "goes googoo at the sight of dragons" trait is therefore
   also her one piece of genuine professional expertise, not a quirk bolted
   on for charm.
6. **Hook/Resolution mirror (per story-structure.md's requirement):** the
   hook shows Wren closed off from *people* specifically (magically fine,
   socially avoidant — comfortable opening toward a retort or a shed scale,
   not toward a person). The resolution shows Wren performing the first
   full, unguarded, person-directed opening of her life, at real risk to
   herself, to anchor her sister against Dissolution. This is the literal
   opposite of the antagonist's Severance and is how the book answers "how
   open do I become."
7. **Title: working title is "The Open Hand."** Picked over reusing
   "Riverbed," which is already the Council's institutional name in-world
   and would read as derivative on a cover.

## Scope decision for this drafting pass (recorded for the user, not a
   silent shortfall)

A full run of this pipeline (20 chapters through Phase 4 audit and Phase 5
scoring) is a multi-hour, multi-pass undertaking even before human review.
This pass delivers a complete Phase 0–2 foundation (everything needed to
draft the whole book) plus genuine drafted prose for the opening movement —
Chapters 1–3, carrying the book through its Hook and into Plot Turn 1 — so
the user has both the full architecture and a real sample of the finished
voice, rather than an outline-only "begin drafting." PROJECT_STATE.yaml
marks exactly which chapters are drafted prose vs. outline-only beats so
nothing is misrepresented as further along than it is.
