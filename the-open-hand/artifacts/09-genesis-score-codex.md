## Evidence by Dimension

### Originality — 9.1 (unchanged)
The core premise — a matriarchy built causally from the magic system, and a protagonist who is explicitly not the badass-warrior archetype — is untouched by the expansion, and the audit's Pass 1 and Pass 7 confirm nothing about the inciting incident, the worldbuilding logic, or the central conceit has been diluted by added scene material. The "uncool, dorky heroine" comic register that made this premise distinctive remains sustained into Movements III and IV, with the expansion itself adding further instances (Ch.17's Bartholomew monologue-to-an-empty-kitchen, Ch.20's "furious, not dying" exchange) rather than thinning it. Held flat rather than raised because the underlying asset and its distribution across the book are unchanged from Iteration 2 — the expansion deepened existing scenes rather than extending the device into new territory (e.g., Council- or Court-facing scenes), which is the same ceiling noted last iteration.

### Theme — 8.6 (was 8.5)
The open-hand / closed-fist / cupped-hand symbol system and the running-water-versus-still-water motif both remain genuinely load-bearing, and the audit explicitly verifies both as intentional, escalating motifs rather than accidental repetition (Pass 6's "correctly excluded" list). The score moves up slightly because the expansion adds the door-left-open ritual's second instance (Ch.19) with the text itself naming the repetition as deliberate ("the same words she'd used the first time, because some promises were worth keeping in the same shape twice") — a third thematic throughline, alongside water and the hand-symbol, that the expansion strengthened rather than diluted. Not raised further because Ch.9's narrated thesis-statement immediately following the fully-dramatized Bleed sequence is still present verbatim, per Pass 3 — the same over-trust-of-restatement note carried unchanged across all three iterations.

### Characters — 8.6 (was 8.4)
Per the audit's Pass 4, all of Iteration 1's originally-failing findings remain resolved with no reversion, and the expansion adds real further texture rather than diluting it: Wren's Ch.5 jealousy admission now sits inside a longer, more textured scene (Hesper's "dye-set" theory of trained selflessness) that gives it more surrounding weight; Bram gains a second, more concrete friction beat in Ch.20 (visible discomfort and a "little stiffly" delivered correction after unintentionally correcting Wren) that is closer to a real, costly moment than Iteration 2's single beat of restraint. Bodily reality is further improved by new material across Ch.17-19 (lingering hand tremor, appetite disruption, involuntary flinch, knee-ache, "thirst so total it was almost its own kind of pain"). Not raised higher because, per the audit, Bram still never costs Wren anything directly, and bodily/physical reality remains concentrated around crisis sequences rather than ordinary daily life — the same residual softness noted, and not prioritized, in both prior iterations.

### Prose — 8.1 (was 8.7, down)
This is the dimension the expansion cost the most, and the score must reflect that rather than letting word-count volume average it out. Iteration 2 found and fixed two instances of the "filed [it] away" tic and confirmed zero remaining instances manuscript-wide. Per the audit's Pass 6a, a fresh search this iteration finds the tic has recurred independently in **five new locations** (Ch.7, 11, 12, 13, 18) — clear evidence that four separately-working teams each reached for the same easy verbal shorthand to dramatize Wren's genuinely load-bearing habit of deferring her feelings, recreating the exact defect Iteration 2 spent effort removing. Per Pass 6b, two further beat-level patterns recur across movements: the "smelled of X and, underneath that, Y" room-atmosphere opening (seven-plus instances, several decorative and interchangeable) and the "small unnecessary task during emotional overwhelm" beat (six instances, with Ch.16 explicitly naming the pattern as universal in the narration itself — prose visibly noticing its own device). None of this is severe enough to fail Pass 6 outright, and the underlying craft elsewhere — sentence-level control, the magic-system prose, the dialogue work — is undiminished or improved. But five recurred phrase-tics plus two cross-movement generic-texture templates are real, locatable craft defects introduced by this specific revision pass, not stylistic preference, and the score must move down to reflect that the same volume that fixed Market also reintroduced a defect this manuscript had already paid to fix once.

### Pacing — 8.6 (was 8.4)
Per the audit's Pass 5, both items resolved in Iteration 2 (Ch.19's mid-rescue near-miss, Ch.12's reciprocity-refusal friction) remain resolved and unchanged, and the expansion adds a genuine further strengthening: the Cae subplot's Ch.20 hearing now nearly fails on the page, with Master Aldric's attack on Wren's credibility through her association with Vesper's Severance history landing as a direct, costly callback to Iris's Ch.14 warning, and the expansion adds a full beat of Wren realizing in real time how thoroughly her own history has become someone else's weapon — sharpening stakes rather than softening them. Raised because the Cae-hearing strengthening is real, new, and load-bearing; not raised further because no work was done on the three carried-over softness notes.

### Market — 8.5 (was 7.4, below 8.0 — Iteration 2's weakest dimension)
The premise's positioning strength is unchanged and remains genuine: a non-warrior, non-conventionally-heroic lead in a causally-built matriarchal secondary world remains a real, specific, currently underserved gap in adult fantasy. The dimension clears 8.0 for the first time because the manuscript is no longer a severe outlier on length: at ~94,780 words it sits at 94.8% of the 100,000-word adult-fantasy target, squarely within normal range for the category, and — critically — the audit confirms this growth is earned text rather than padding inserted to hit a number: the same scene-deepening work that drove Theme, Characters, Pacing, Coherence, and Opening upward is the same work that closed the word-count gap. Not scored higher than 8.5 because the same audit that confirms the growth is mostly earned also documents specific instances where added length manifested as repeated, lower-effort filler (the Pass 6a/6b findings) rather than fully differentiated new material — a discerning agent reading closely would notice the same recurring textures Pass 6 catalogued, which is a real, if minor, drag on a manuscript being positioned as polished and submission-ready at full length.

### Voice — 8.3 (was 8.9, down — new weakest-tied dimension)
This score must absorb the real cost of the audit's most consequential new finding. Per Pass 2, the differentiation rules established in Iteration 1 and verified clean in Iteration 2 hold for every secondary character under significantly heavier new dialogue load (Cae, Corvin, Hesper, Iris, and Mari were all re-checked across their expanded Ch.14-20 material and show no drift toward a shared rhetorical fingerprint). But Vesper's exclusive hedge-then-antithesis construction — the single most distinctive voice marker in the book, and the marker whose exclusivity was twice explicitly verified clean (Iteration 1's correction, Iteration 2's confirmation) — has now leaked into Wren's dialogue twice in Ch.17, in the same scene, with one instance explicitly naming the borrowing ("the way you'd say it"). This is not a matter of interpretation: it is a direct, locatable violation of a rule this manuscript has been audited against twice before and held clean both times. Two lines in one chapter is narrow, not pervasive — it does not fail Pass 2 outright, and it does not approach Iteration 1's severity, where an eleven-year-old spoke in adult analytical hedging throughout. But the construction's entire value as a voice marker depends on its exclusivity, and even an isolated leak measurably dilutes it. Per the explicit instruction governing this re-score, new prose must be held to the same prose-quality bar as original text regardless of volume, and this is exactly the kind of finding that bar exists to catch. Scored below Iteration 2's 8.9 specifically because this dimension went from a clean, twice-verified pass to a pass-with-a-real-exception under the same scrutiny.
