## Evidence by Dimension

### Originality — 9.1 (unchanged)

### Theme — 8.9 (was 8.6, up +0.3 — the largest move this iteration)
Per the audit's Pass 3, the single specific defect that had capped this dimension at 8.6 across all four prior iterations — Ch.9's narrated thesis-statement immediately following the dramatized Bleed sequence — is now confirmed cleanly resolved. The replacement passage (*"Some still-functioning part of Wren's mind tried, out of pure professional habit, to log it... and got no further than three words before the rest of her gave out entirely"*) dramatizes the protagonist's own measuring instinct failing in real time, rather than a narrator stepping outside the scene to restate its meaning after the fact; the reflection that follows is gated through Wren's own in-scene noticing ("it occurred to her") and is consistent with her established cataloguing characterization rather than a new device. This is judged a full resolution, not a relocation of the same problem, because the over-explanation pattern specifically named — stating outright what a dramatized scene has already shown — is genuinely absent from the new text; what remains is interiority that is itself dramatized action (a habit breaking down under load), which is a different and acceptable thing. The open-hand/closed-fist symbol system and the running-water motif remain independently confirmed intentional and unaffected. Not raised above 8.9 because this is recovery of a long-standing, single named defect rather than the addition of new thematic territory beyond what the symbol system already carried.

### Characters — 8.7 (was 8.6, up +0.1 — a deliberately modest move)
Per the audit's detailed Pass 5/Verdict assessment, the new Ch.20 material gives Bram a direct, plainly-spoken admission that the slow pace of the relationship costs him something, and gives Wren a corresponding acknowledgment to Hesper that she is knowingly accepting that cost. This is a real, voice-consistent improvement (confirmed under Pass 2 to stay inside Bram's established clipped, plain register) and is credited as such. However, read against the audit's exact carried finding — "Bram never costs Wren anything directly" — the new scene does not relocate any cost onto Wren's side of the ledger: what is shown costing something is Bram's patience, and what Wren does is *absorb* that information, not *pay* anything herself. No scene anywhere in the manuscript shows Wren losing something, being inconvenienced, or sacrificing anything as a direct consequence of the relationship. This dimension is raised only modestly, reflecting a genuine but partial and narrower-than-claimed resolution — the finding is narrowed (cost is now named and visible, where before it was invisible) rather than closed. All previously-credited Pass 4 material (Wren's Ch.5 jealousy, the bodily-reality beats, the comic register) remains present and unaffected. Not raised further because the dimension's most concrete, repeatedly-named lever was not fully executed, and this score should not credit it as though it were.
