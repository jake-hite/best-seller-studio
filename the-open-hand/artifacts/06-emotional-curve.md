## High-impact beats (full list, for quick reference during drafting)

1. Mari's collapse (Ch.2) — inciting incident proper.
2. Corvin's refusal (Ch.4) — first real cost of the quest.
3. The wyrm-scale theory clicking (Ch.7) — competence payoff, dork joy under pressure.
4. The Bleed event (Ch.9) — embodied stakes; reader's gut-check.
5. The villain-mirror reveal (Ch.11) — the book's highest-risk swing.
6. The near-Dissolution (Ch.16) — the low point.
7. The convergent-hinge refusal (Ch.18) — the thematic argument resolved in action, not dialogue.
8. The Open Hand (Ch.19) — the climax the whole emotional curve has been built to earn.

## Abandonment-risk summary

The two highest-risk stretches are **Ch.3–4** (information-gathering can stall
momentum right after the hook) and **Ch.10–14** (the classic sagging middle).
Both stretches are deliberately weighted with high-impact reversals (Ch.4's
guild refusal, Ch.11's midpoint reveal, Ch.13's joy grace-note) so no more
than two chapters pass without either a reversal or a new piece of forward
pressure.
